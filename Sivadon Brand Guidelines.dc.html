Proportion & contrast

Roughly 65% neutral, 20% Mono 100, 10% green, 5% orange.

One correction to make. White text on Sivadon Green reads 2.8:1 and on Orange 2.5:1 — both fail AA at any size, and the live site does this on its green and Submit buttons. Set button labels in Mono 100 instead: 6.3:1 on green, 7.1:1 on orange. Green as text on white is 2.8:1, so reserve it for 24px and above.

Product status colours — Success #00875F, Warning #B26A00, Error #D0342C — appear in interfaces only, never in marketing.

04 · Spacing, grid & components

An 8px base unit. Every margin, padding, gap and component height is a multiple of it. If a value isn't on the scale, it's a mistake.

Scale

8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96 · 144

Section padding 96 desktop / 48 mobile. Card padding 24. Gap between cards 20.

Grid

Desktop ≥1280 — 12 col, 24 gutter, 64 margin
Tablet 992–1279 — 8 col, 20 gutter, 40 margin
Mobile <992 — 4 col, 16 gutter, 20 margin
Max content width 1280px.

Buttons

Contact us Find out more Download rate card Disabled

One Orange primary per view — that is the conversion button. Green is the secondary for anything repeated, outline is tertiary. Labels set in Mono 100, not white. Height 48px, 44px minimum on mobile. Radius 16px on buttons, inputs and cards; 24px on pills — never mixed within one component.

Iconography

One family, outline only: 1.75px stroke on a 24px grid, round caps and joins, no fills, no two-tone. 24px in interfaces, 40px in marketing, always Mono 100 or Sivadon 80. Orange is reserved for the single call to action, never for icons. The site's mixed set of sourced SVGs should be replaced with a single licensed family.
