Sivadon Logistics joins Amilo

Thailand’s nationwide logistics partner becomes part of Amilo’s Southeast Asian network. Same team, same service, and a much wider reach for our customers.

In August 2024, Sivadon Logistics became part of Amilo, the Singapore-based logistics technology group. For the businesses we serve across Thailand, the day-to-day stays the same: the same team, the same warehouses, the same delivery network. What changes is how far that network can now reach.

A new chapter for Sivadon

For more than 20 years, Sivadon has moved goods for local and international brands across all 77 provinces of Thailand. We built that network one route, one warehouse and one customer at a time, and we’ve always run it with our own people and our own systems.

Joining Amilo connects that network to a group building logistics infrastructure across Southeast Asia. Sivadon keeps its name, its team and its way of working. We now do it with the backing, technology and regional reach of a larger partner.

Why we chose Amilo

Amilo pairs operations with technology, which is how we’ve always run Sivadon. Their teams understand what it takes to move freight on time in markets that are growing fast, and they see Thailand as central to that growth rather than a stop along the way.

For our clients expanding beyond Thailand, and for regional brands expanding into it, that shared focus matters. It means one partner who understands both sides of the border.

What it means for our customers

Nothing changes in how you work with us today. Your account team, contracts and contact numbers all stay as they are. Over time, you’ll see the benefits of the wider network:

  • Nationwide delivery continues across all provinces, at the same service levels.
  • Warehousing and fulfilment keeps running on our in-house systems, with 100% inventory accuracy.
  • Cross-border shipping connects to Amilo’s regional network, for brands moving goods into and out of Thailand.
  • Technology investment in tracking, reporting and integrations, shared across the group.

What happens next

We’ll bring the two businesses together gradually and carefully, without disrupting the shipments you rely on. As new services and routes become available, we’ll share them here first and tell existing customers directly.

If you have questions about what this means for your account, speak to your Sivadon contact or call our hotline on 096-798-8971.
